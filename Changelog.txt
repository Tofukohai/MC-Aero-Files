A big one — the render stack got rebuilt around Sodium 0.8 / Sable 2.0, shaders are back, and the
distant-terrain LOD system returned. On top of that, 51 mods were updated and two long-standing
startup/rendering bugs were fixed with custom builds.

## ⚙️ Platform

- **NeoForge**: 21.1.219 → **21.1.248** (required by JEI 19.43+, which needs ≥ 21.1.238)
- Minecraft stays on 1.21.1

## 🔧 Custom Patch Builds

Two mods ship as locally-patched jars because the upstream releases are broken and the fixes
aren't merged yet. Both are still fully in the pack — they just no longer auto-update.

- **Copper Age Backport** [0.1.4-aeropatch] — upstream 0.1.4 crashes on startup. It re-registers
  `minecraft:armor_material/copper` on every access, which aborts NeoForge's item registration
  mid-dispatch and takes unrelated mods down with it (My Nether's Delight, Create Aeronautics, and
  a misleading "Sodium config not found" crash were all collateral). Patched to register once.
  Upstream issues #101/#107 are open; fix PRs #106/#108 are unmerged.
- **Voxy** [0.2.15-beta-aero] — fixes transparent diagonal seams that appeared on the south face of
  distant grass blocks. Voxy bakes each block face with a software rasterizer that splits the quad
  into two triangles; pixels landing exactly on the shared diagonal were rejected by both halves and
  left fully transparent. South was the only face affected because it's the one bake angle with no
  rotation applied, so its maths lands on exact zeros instead of being nudged off by rounding.

## ❌ Removed Mods

- **What Are They Up To (Watut)** — crashed on startup with Iris installed; its blur shader can't
  resolve a vanilla shader file. Unfixed upstream and no newer NeoForge build exists.
- **Sodium Dynamic Lights** — superseded by LambDynamicLights on the Sodium 0.8 stack
- **Sodium Options API** — folded into Reese's Sodium Options 2.0
- **Create Sable Dynamic Lights** — incompatible with the new render stack
- **Iris & Oculus Flywheel Compat** — not compatible with the current Iris/Flywheel builds
- **Analog Audio**, **Create Live Radio** — replaced by Glaiden's Audio
- **Solas Shader** — dropped, no Colorwheel patch available
- **End Sea Iris Fix** (in-house) — the fix stopped working against current Iris and was retired
- **Sable Xaero Bridge** — superseded by Create - Xaero's map

## 🎛️ Config & Defaults

- **Sky Archipelago**: island overlap mode changed from `VOID` to `CRATER` — removes the harsh
  jagged edges where two islands intersect
- **Grassier Grass**: now ships a pack default with the segmented (vanilla-like) blade style
- Shaderpacks are pinned to Colorwheel-patchable builds, so **Photon** (1.3b → 1.3a) and
  **Complementary Reimagined** (r5.8.1 → r5.6.1) intentionally read as downgrades — they are the
  newest versions Colorwheel can currently patch.
